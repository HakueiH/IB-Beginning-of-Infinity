    Chapter 1 · The Reach of Explanations

    Hard to varyreaches far

    A good explanation is hard to vary, so it reaches beyond what it was made to explain.

    How it works

    Move one partand seewhat breaks

    A myth bends to fit anything you change. A good explanation breaks when you move one part, and that is what makes it good.

    The myth: Persephone spends part of each year below ground, and her mother's grief makes winter.

    The tilt: the Earth's axis leans, so each hemisphere takes turns facing the Sun more directly.

    The tilt, in 3D

    Real life

    Neptune. Uranus drifted off its predicted path. The laws of gravity said something unseen was pulling it, and in 1846 astronomers found Neptune almost where the sums said.
