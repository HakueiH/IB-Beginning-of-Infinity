    Chapter 10 · A Dream of Socrates

    No authoritynot even a god

    No authority can make an idea true, not even a god; you learn by criticising ideas, not deferring to people.

    How it works

    Who sitson thethrone?

    No authority makes an idea true, not even a god. Put an argument forward and let criticism try to knock it down. The throne stays empty.

    "This idea is true, because…"

    The throne: empty.

    In 3D

    The emptythrone

    Turn it any way. The throne stays empty.

    No authority sits here. Turn it and look.

    Real life

    The oracle. Chaerephon asked the oracle at Delphi if anyone was wiser than Socrates. The answer, no, started him questioning everyone.

    The trial. In 399 BC Athens sentenced Socrates to death for impiety and corrupting the young. The arguments outlived the verdict.
