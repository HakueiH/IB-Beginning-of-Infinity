    Chapter 11 · The Multiverse

    The weirdnessis the reach

    Take quantum theory seriously and you get many parallel histories; the weirdness is a sign the explanation reaches further.

    How it works

    One photontwo slits

    Send light through two slits one photon at a time. Each lands as a single dot, yet the dots build stripes. Each photon goes through one slit; its instances in other universes go through the other, and they interfere with it.

    In 3D

    Two slits,one dotat a time

    No particles yet.

    Particles go through one at a time. Watch the screen behind.

    Real life

    One at a time. In 1989 a lab fired electrons through a double slit one at a time. Single dots still built up the stripes.

    Quantum computers. Shor showed in 1994 that a quantum computer could factor huge numbers fast, in a way only many histories at once explain well.
