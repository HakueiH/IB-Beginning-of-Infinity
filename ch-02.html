    Chapter 2 · Closer to Reality

    We seethroughtheories

    We never see reality directly; we see it through theories, and even instruments only work because of those theories.

    How it works

    Take awaythe theory

    A telescope image is only Jupiter and its moons because of the theory you read it with. Strip the theory away and it is light on a sensor.

    Real life

    Jupiter's moons. In 1610 a telescope showed four points of light that moved with Jupiter. Only a theory of orbits made them moons.

    The faint hiss. In 1965 a radio antenna picked up a hiss from every direction. Read through cosmology, it was the afterglow of the early universe.

    Thomas Edison

    1847–1931 · American inventor

    Said his wins came from trial after trial. Deutsch says Edison misread himself. It was ideas, not grind.
