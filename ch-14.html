    Chapter 14 · Why Are Flowers Beautiful?

    Beautyis real

    Flowers had to signal across species, so their beauty can't be just one species' taste. Beauty is objective.

    How it works

    Same flowertwo eyes

    Bees see ultraviolet. Many flowers carry patterns only a bee can see, pointing the way in. The flower is signalling across species.

    In 3D

    Oneflower

    Made to be read by another species, so it had to meet a standard outside its own.

    Turn it. It was shaped to be seen by another species.

    Real life

    Nectar guides. Many flowers carry patterns in ultraviolet that bees can see and we can't, pointing to the nectar.

    Red for birds. Flowers pollinated by hummingbirds are often red, a colour bees see poorly.
