    Infinityis not a wall

    Infinity isn't a wall; we can understand it, and the paradoxes are where we learn.

    How it works

    The hotelis fullcome in

    Every room in Hilbert's hotel is taken, and there are infinitely many rooms. Ask everyone to move, and there is always room for more.

      In 3D

      A fullhotel

      Every room is full.

      Every room is taken, and the corridor never ends.

      Real life

      Achilles and the tortoise. Zeno said the runner can never catch up. Infinitely many steps can still add up to a finite distance.

      Bigger infinities. Cantor showed in 1891 that no list can hold every infinite decimal. Some infinities are bigger than others.
