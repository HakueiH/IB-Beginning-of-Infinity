    Chapter 16 · The Evolution of Creativity

    First we copiedthen webroke out

    Creativity first evolved to copy memes faithfully; breaking out of that copying makes progress possible.

    How it works

    You seethe actionnot the idea

    A meme is never seen directly, only someone's actions. To copy it faithfully you have to guess the reason behind them. Even faithful copying takes creativity.

    Real life

    Hand axes. Stone hand axes changed very little for over a million years. Faithful copying, almost no new ideas.

    Apes copy steps. Byrne found that gorillas learn to prepare nettles as a sequence of steps, not by copying each motion.

    In 3D

    Watchthe tap

    Drag round the bowl · look for the reason

    You can see the tap from every side. You still can't see the reason.

    An egg is tapped on the rim of a bowl. Look from any side before you guess.

    People in this chapter

    Richard Byrne

    British primatologist at St Andrews

    Showed apes copy by breaking actions into steps. A clue to what creativity first evolved for.
