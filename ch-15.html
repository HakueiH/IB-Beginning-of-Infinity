    Chapter 15 · The Evolution of Culture

    Static societiessilence critics

    Static societies survive by shutting down criticism; dynamic ones survive by changing. Anti-rational memes spread by disabling their holders' criticism; rational memes spread by surviving it.

    How it works

    Let acritic speak

    Memes are ideas that get themselves copied. A rational meme survives because people who think about it critically still choose to keep it. An anti-rational meme survives by disabling its holders' critical faculties. Static societies are held still by anti-rational memes.

    No one has spoken. The two ideas look alike.

    Real life

    Nullius in verba. Founded in 1660, the Royal Society soon took as its motto 'take nobody's word for it'.

    Three thousand years of style. Ancient Egyptian art kept the same rules for figures for roughly three thousand years.
