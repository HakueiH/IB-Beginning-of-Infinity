    Chapter 5 · The Reality of Abstractions

    Ideas are realthey causethings

    Numbers, laws and ideas are real, because they explain and they cause things.

    How it works

    Switch offthe idea

    An arch is just stones until the idea of the arch holds them. The idea is not a stone, and it still holds the bridge up.

    Real life

    No largest prime. A proof over 2,000 years old shows the primes never run out. No one has ever seen all of them, and the fact is still true.

    Clocks in orbit. Satellite navigation clocks are adjusted for relativity. Without that abstract correction, positions would drift.

    Source: Wikipedia, “Error analysis for the Global Positioning System”.
