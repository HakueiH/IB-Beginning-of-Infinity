    Chapter 4 · Creation

    Designwithouta designer

    Living things look designed. Paley said a watch means a watchmaker. Darwin showed another way: variation and selection make design with no designer.

    Replicators

    Copied,then selected

    Generation 1. Every shape is a variant.

    A gene is a replicator: it causes its environment to copy it. Each shell's shape is set by its genes. Each generation, most shells crack. The survivors' genes are copied into the next, with small errors.

    Lamarck vs Darwin

    Gained in life,or copied?

    Lamarck thought creatures pass on what they gain in their lives: instruction from the environment. Neo-Darwinism says the young inherit only the genes, and new designs come from random errors in copying them, not from the creature's own efforts.

    Its wall thickened in its lifetime. Its young are born plain.
