    Chapter 6 · The Jump to Universality

    Step by stepthen no limit

    Systems that improve step by step eventually jump to unlimited reach, as alphabets, computers and minds did.

    How it works

    Add a letterthen anotherthen anything

    Pictures add one word per symbol. Letters creep along, then suddenly the set can spell any word, including words nobody has made up yet.

    In 3D

    Steps,then a jump

    Each step adds one more thing.

    Each step adds one more thing a system can do. Then one more rule makes it universal.

    Real life

    Movable type. Around 1450, a small set of metal letters in Europe could print any text at all.

    A machine on paper. Babbage designed the Analytical Engine in the 1830s. It was never finished, but on paper it could compute anything.

    People in this chapter

    Charles Babbage

    1791–1871 · English mathematician

    Designed a universal computer a century before anyone built one.
